# Sources for the analytics skills

The analytics skills in this folder (forecasting, statistical-tests, anomaly-detection, trend-and-seasonality, driver-analysis) were written for Intelligence Corner in October 2026 (Quick 004). Before writing them we surveyed published data-analytics agent skills. No text or code was copied from any of them: the skills are in our own words and code. The methods below were adopted from them, and credit for them goes to these sources.

| Source | License | Methods adopted |
|---|---|---|
| apoorva2121/data-scientist-skills (ds-forecasting, ds-outliers) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Apoorva Dixit | Rolling-origin backtest with several origins spaced a full horizon apart; keep a complex model only when it beats the simple baseline by more than the origin-to-origin noise; check that prediction intervals cover what they claim, and widen them from backtest misses when they do not; zero versus missing in gaps; modified z-score at 3.5; sorting flags into data error, genuine extreme or different population. |
| K-Dense-AI/scientific-agent-skills (statistical-analysis, statsmodels, scikit-learn) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 K-Dense Inc. | Choosing a test by the question rather than by a normality pretest; Welch by default; Mann-Whitney read as a distribution comparison; effect-size conventions (Hedges g, epsilon squared, Cramer V by degrees of freedom); no observed power; HC3 and diagnostics for OLS; permutation importance on held-out rows. |
| anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (data/statistical-analysis, finance/variance-analysis, small-business) | Apache-2.0 | Give a range, not a point; stock-outs are not zero demand; too-wide bands reported as too noisy rather than as a number; materiality floors; start value plus drivers equals end value. Their three-way price, volume and mix formula does not reconcile, so driver-analysis uses a per-segment volume, mix and rate split that does. |
| nimrodfisher/data-analytics-skills (time-series-analysis, root-cause-investigation) | MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Nimrod Fisher | Seasonal strength above 0.6 makes raw period-over-period comparisons misleading; pitfalls of week-on-week, month-on-month and year-on-year comparisons; a cause must explain the timing, the size and the units affected. |
| borghei/Claude-Skills (statistical-analyst) | MIT with Commons Clause (not open source) | Idea only, not text: judge a difference against the smallest change worth acting on, not only against zero. |

Rejected: libraries the sandbox does not have or cannot run within 512 MB and no network (Pingouin, R, PyMC, Prophet, TimesFM and other foundation models, aeon, ruptures, pmdarima); fixed single 80/20 splits; plain z-scores at 2 to 2.5, which raise several false alarms a year on normal data.
